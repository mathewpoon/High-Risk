### Overview of the Infraorbital Nerve

The infraorbital nerve is a significant branch of the maxillary nerve, which is the second division of the trigeminal nerve (V2). It plays a crucial role in providing sensory innervation to various facial regions, including the lower eyelid, cheek, side of the nose, and upper lip [Data: Entities (2199); Relationships (7893)].
