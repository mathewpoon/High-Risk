The correct answer is **A. Collagen**.

### Explanation

**Collagen** is a structural, fibrous protein that is abundantly found in the dermis of the skin. It provides strength and elasticity to the skin, making it a crucial component of the connective tissue. Collagen fibers form a network that supports the skin's structure and helps maintain its firmness and resilience.

**Heparin** is not a structural protein; it is a glycosaminoglycan that acts as an anticoagulant, preventing blood clots.

**Lipocyte**, also known as an adipocyte, is a type of cell that stores fat, not a protein.

**Melanin** is a pigment produced by melanocytes in the skin, responsible for skin color, but it is not a structural protein.

Collagen's role in the dermis is essential for maintaining the skin's integrity and function, making it the correct choice among the options provided.
