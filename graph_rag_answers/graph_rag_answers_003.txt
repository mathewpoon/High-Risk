### Ectomesenchyme

Ectomesenchyme is a type of mesenchyme that is derived from neural crest cells. Neural crest cells are a group of cells that emerge from the embryonic ectoderm layer and migrate to various parts of the embryo, where they differentiate into a diverse range of cell types and structures. Ectomesenchyme is particularly involved in the formation of craniofacial structures, parts of the peripheral nervous system, and some other specialized tissues.
