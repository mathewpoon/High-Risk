### Innervation of the Soft Palate Muscles

The innervation of the muscles of the soft palate is primarily provided by the vagus nerve, with a specific exception. The vagus nerve, also known as Cranial Nerve X, is responsible for innervating all the muscles of the soft palate except for one: the tensor veli palatini muscle. This particular muscle is innervated by a branch of the mandibular nerve, which is a division of the trigeminal nerve (Cranial Nerve V) [Data: Entities (1115, 1109); Relationships (6914); Sources (534, 825)].
